Shape & space
Radii, v2: 6–8px labels · 10–12px buttons and inputs · 0–4px photography · 20px reserved for the two or three soft color blocks that genuinely need to be containers (hero, about, newsletter). Nothing at 28px any more; no circles, no full pills.
Containers are earned: most sections are separated by whitespace and a 1px #DCD4C2 hairline rather than a rounded rectangle. If two rounded blocks would sit next to each other, one of them becomes plain cream.
Rhythm: 40px page gutters, 20–26px between siblings, 72–88px between sections. One full-bleed dark section per page.

Photography
Property and architecture: 0–4px corners, large, never smaller than a third of the section. The photograph is the loudest thing on a listing, not the type.
Danielle and editorial: may take the 20px soft corner. That difference is deliberate — soft corners mean personality, square corners mean property.
Rules: natural light, no filters, no duotone, no gradient scrims. Vary proportion between neighbors instead of adding treatments. Nothing floats on top of a photo except a real, useful label.
do
Let one family own a section · write dark-on-light within that family · keep the Danielle mark lowercase · put burgundy on prices and the word you want remembered · separate most sections with whitespace and a hairline · let photography be large, square and the loudest thing on the page.
don't
Stack three color families in one block · set Rozha One in paragraphs · use burgundy as a background · add a fifth typeface · wrap every section in a rounded card · give every photograph the same corner · float badges over listing photos · put drop shadows or gradients on anything · use circles or 999px pills.
